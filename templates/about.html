The artist behind the sound

A lifetime in every note.

Ijhayclassics brings curiosity, discipline and a deep love of African music to every project.

01 — The journey

Built by ear. Refined by purpose.

Renowned for his versatility, Okunade Israel Oluwasegun, professionally known as Ijhayclassics, began his musical journey by teaching himself piano without a tutor. Early years of trial, error and relentless practice shaped him into a confident intermediate pianist within three years.

Eight years into his piano journey, his passion led him to guitar and music production. Through years of dedication, creativity and continuous growth, he evolved into a versatile musician: pianist, guitarist, producer and music director, collaborating with artists to bring ambitious musical visions to life.

02 — The sound

Rooted in Africa. Open to everything.

Every song begins with a vision and ends with excellence. Ijhayclassics creates authentic, emotionally impactful records for emerging artists, worship leaders, bands and established performers.

The production style draws deeply from African rhythm, melody and culture—spanning Afrobeats, Afro-fusion, Afro-pop, Highlife, Reggae, Jazz, Gospel, Contemporary Worship, R&B and Soul. From the first melody to the final master, every detail is shaped to honour the artist and connect with listeners.

Let’s create music that inspires, moves and lives beyond the moment.
